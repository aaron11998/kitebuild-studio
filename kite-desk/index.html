How it works

  1. 1

    Set up. We load your hours, services, prices and FAQs into a simple config.

  2. 2

    Connect. Kite Desk is connected to your WhatsApp Business number via the WhatsApp Cloud API.

  3. 3

    Reply. Customers get instant answers and bookings; anything tricky is handed to your team.

Features

FAQ answers

Hours, prices, location and policies, answered from your own business info.

Booking flow

Collects name, service and preferred time, then confirms back to the customer.

Human handoff

Customers can ask for a person at any time; your team takes over the chat.

Your data, your config

Business info lives in a config file you can review and update.

Yes. Handoff to a human is a core feature.
